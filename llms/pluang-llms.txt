# Pluang

> Pluang is an Indonesian multi-asset investment and trading platform. From a single mobile app, retail users invest in US stocks and ETFs (fractional from $1), Indonesian stocks, cryptocurrencies with staking, mutual funds (Reksa Dana), physical gold and silver, options, and leveraged crypto futures. It operates under OJK and Bappebti regulatory oversight and reports 12M+ users. Pluang publishes no public developer API; this file indexes its public web, help, legal, and security surface.

## Product
- [Pluang home](https://pluang.com/): Multi-asset investment app overview and downloads.
- [Web trading](https://trade.pluang.com/login): Browser trading platform (login/sign up).
- [Help Center](https://help.pluang.com/): Support and FAQ.
